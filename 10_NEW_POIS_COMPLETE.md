## New POIs Added (IDs 280-289)

### 1. Theatre of Thorikos (ID 280)
- **Coordinates:** 37.73806, 24.05375
- **Period:** Archaic (525-480 BC)
- **Significance:** **World's oldest known stone theater** with unique elongated (non-semicircular) shape
- **Location:** Laurion mining district, southern Attica
- **Image:** AI-generated (`theatre_thorikos.jpg`)

### 2. Theatre of Ikarion (ID 281)
- **Coordinates:** 38.095558, 23.900489
- **Period:** 6th century BC onwards
- **Significance:** **Legendary birthplace of Greek tragedy** where Thespis created the first actor
- **Cultural Impact:** Origin of the word "thespian"
- **Image:** AI-generated (`theatre_ikarion.jpg`)

### 3. Plato's Academy (ID 282)
- **Coordinates:** 37.99250, 23.70806
- **Period:** Founded 387 BC
- **Significance:** **First institution of higher learning in the Western world**
- **Students:** Aristotle studied here for 20 years
- **Duration:** Operated for ~900 years (until 529 AD)
- **Image:** AI-generated (`plato_academy.jpg`)

### 4. Aristotle's Lyceum (ID 283)
- **Coordinates:** 37.9740750, 23.7435028
- **Period:** Founded 335 BC
- **Significance:** **Birthplace of the scientific method** and empirical research
- **Name Origin:** "Peripatetic" = walking around while teaching
- **Discovery:** Only discovered in 1996, opened to public 2009
- **Image:** AI-generated (`aristotle_lyceum.jpg`)

### 5. Daphni Monastery (ID 284)
- **Coordinates:** 38.01296, 23.63588
- **Period:** 11th century AD
- **Significance:** **UNESCO World Heritage Site** with exceptional Byzantine mosaics
- **Art:** Supreme examples of middle Byzantine gold-ground mosaic art
- **Location:** Sacred Way to Eleusis
- **Image:** AI-generated (`daphni_monastery.jpg`)

### 6. Kaisariani Monastery (ID 285)
- **Coordinates:** 37.96083, 23.79833
- **Period:** ca. 1100 AD (on ancient sanctuary site)
- **Significance:** Byzantine monastery built on ancient Aphrodite temple site
- **Setting:** Mount Hymettus pine forests with sacred spring
- **Frescoes:** Late 17th century paintings (1682)
- **Image:** AI-generated (`kaisariani_monastery.jpg`)

### 7. Brauron Sanctuary of Artemis (ID 286)
- **Coordinates:** 37.92611, 23.99361
- **Period:** 8th-3rd century BC
- **Significance:** **Most important Artemis sanctuary in Greece**
- **Ritual:** Arkteia - girls aged 5-10 "playing the bear" in coming-of-age ceremony
- **Unique Feature:** Greece's only surviving Classical stone bridge
- **Image:** AI-generated (`brauron_sanctuary.jpg`)

### 8. Ancient Oropos (ID 287)
- **Coordinates:** 38.319500, 23.790000
- **Period:** Archaic through Roman periods
- **Significance:** **Strategic border city** constantly contested by Athens and Thebes
- **Geography:** Controlled vital crossing to Euboea and Athens' grain route
- **Harbor:** Delphinion at Asopos River mouth
- **Image:** AI-generated (`oropos_city.jpg`)

### 9. Ancient Megara (ID 288)
- **Coordinates:** 37.99639, 23.34444
- **Period:** Dorian city-state, peak 8th-7th century BC
- **Significance:** **Founded Byzantium and Chalcedon** - major colonizing power
- **Monument:** Theagenes Fountain (5th century BC) with octagonal columns
- **Rivalry:** Conflict with Athens over Salamis triggered tensions leading to Peloponnesian War
- **Image:** AI-generated (`ancient_megara.jpg`)

### 10. Theatre at Amphiareion (ID 289)
- **Coordinates:** 38.291581, 23.845344
- **Period:** 4th century BC
- **Significance:** Well-preserved theater for dramatic/musical contests at healing sanctuary
- **Festival:** Greater Amphiareia held every 4 years (from 332 BC)
- **Condition:** Exceptionally preserved stone seating
- **Image:** AI-generated (`amphiareion_theatre.jpg`)
