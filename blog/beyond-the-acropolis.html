Beyond the Acropolis: three days with a free map

You can see the Parthenon in a morning and still miss Attica. This outline uses Echoes of Athens as the only paid-tour replacement: walk with GPS in the center, ride a bus or car for the rest. Switch the app to Simulated GPS in the evening to preview tomorrow’s pins.

Day 1 — The classical core

Start on the rock: Parthenon, Erechtheion, Theatre of Dionysus, Odeon of Herodes Atticus. Drop to the Agora and the Temple of Hephaestus, then the Temple of Olympian Zeus and Hadrian’s Arch. Late afternoon: Areopagus, Pnyx, and Philopappos for the light. That cluster is walkable; let the map trigger stories as you go rather than reading a paper list.

Day 2 — Hills, monasteries, forest

East: Kaisariani Monastery and the Hymettus forest. North: Tatoi and, if you have a car or the Parnitha bus, Bafi refuge or Lake Beletsi. Daphni Monastery (UNESCO) sits on the way toward Eleusis if you are heading west instead. This is the day most short-stay visitors skip, and the day that makes Athens feel like a region rather than a museum island.

Day 3 — The riviera to Sounion

Follow Poseidonos south: Glyfada, Kavouri, Vouliagmeni beach and the thermal lake, then Varkiza, Lagonisi, Anavyssos. End at the Temple of Poseidon at Cape Sounion for sunset — the cliché exists because the geometry of sea, columns, and sky still works. If you have a fourth day, swap the riviera for Lavrio and Thorikos, or Schinias and Marathon.

Practical notes

  • Bring headphones. Narration uses your phone’s voice engine; public playback on a bus is rude and muddy.
  • Real GPS drains battery. Download is unnecessary, but a power bank is not.
  • Site opening hours and tickets are still the official ones. The app is a guide, not a gate.
  • Share a pin with Share in the story drawer so a travel partner opens the same POI.

For caves, waterfalls, and fortresses that do not fit a three-day grid, use the hidden gems list and the searchable directory of all 355 sites.
