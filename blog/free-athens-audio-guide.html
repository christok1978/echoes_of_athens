The free Athens audio guide that lives in your browser

Most “Athens audio tours” are either a plastic wand you return at the Acropolis ticket office, or a paid app that wants an account before you hear a sentence about the Parthenon. Echoes of Athens is the other option: a free map of 355 places in Attica, with a story, a photo, and a quiz at each pin.

What you actually get

  • An interactive Leaflet map of Athens, Piraeus, the riviera, Parnitha, Penteli, Marathon, Sounion, and Salamis
  • Spoken narration using the Web Speech API already on your phone — no audio files to download
  • GPS that can be real (walk up to a site) or simulated (tap the map from a cafe or hotel)
  • Short quizzes that award in-app “drachmas”
  • A share link for any pin, so a friend can open the same story

How to start in two minutes

  1. Open echoes-of-athens.vercel.app on your phone.
  2. Stay in Simulated GPS to tap around, or switch to Real GPS when you are on the street.
  3. Tap a gold-ringed pin, then Open Story. Press play for the narration.
  4. Optional: add the site to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone; Install app on Android).

If you already know the site you want, jump straight in with a deep link — for example the Parthenon at index.html?poi=0.

Why it stays free

The guide is a static progressive web app. There is no booking engine in the way, and geolocation is never sent to a server. That keeps hosting cheap and the privacy promise simple: your walk belongs to you.

Use it as a full-day Acropolis-to-Plaka loop, or as a weekend planner for places day tours skip. When you are ready for mountains and sea in the same trip, read Beyond the Acropolis.
