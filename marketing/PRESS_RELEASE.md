# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**Free audio guide maps 309 temples, monasteries, and wild places across Athens and Attica**

ATHENS, Greece — 4 September 2026 — Echoes of Athens, a free progressive web app, now covers 309 points of interest across Attica: classical ruins, Byzantine monasteries and churches, caves, waterfalls, forests, and the Athens Riviera. Visitors open a browser map, walk with GPS or tap to simulate, and hear a researched narration at each pin.

The guide is live at https://echoes-of-athens.vercel.app/ and does not require an app-store download or user account. Geolocation is processed only on the device.

**Why it exists**
Paid bus tours and museum wands cover a handful of headline monuments. Independent travelers are left to stitch Wikipedia, Google Maps, and a paper guidebook. Echoes of Athens puts the pins, the story, a photo, and a short quiz in one place — including sites that rarely appear on a six-hour itinerary, such as the theatre at Thorikos, Aegosthena fortress, Davelis Cave, Kaisariani Monastery, Lake Vouliagmeni, and trails on Mount Parnitha.

**At a glance**
- 309 mapped sites (central Athens through Sounion, Marathon, Parnitha, Penteli, Salamis)
- English spoken narration via the phone’s built-in speech engine
- Quizzes and optional local rewards
- Shareable deep links for any pin
