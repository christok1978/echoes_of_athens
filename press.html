Boilerplate

Echoes of Athens is a free progressive web app that maps 355 temples, ruins, Byzantine churches, monasteries, beaches, caves, and mountain sites across Athens and Attica. Visitors open a map, walk (or simulate GPS), and hear a researched narration at each pin, then take a short quiz. Location never leaves the device. Live at https://echoes-of-athens.vercel.app.

Facts

  • 355 points of interest (IDs 0–354)
  • Coverage: central Athens, Piraeus, the Athens Riviera, Mount Parnitha, Penteli, Hymettus, Marathon, Sounion, Salamis, and inland Attica
  • Languages: English narration via the device speech engine
  • Price: free. No in-app purchases required to hear stories
  • Platform: browser PWA (iOS Safari, Android Chrome, desktop)
  • Privacy: geolocation is processed only on the client
  • Open source: github.com/christok1978/echoes_of_athens
