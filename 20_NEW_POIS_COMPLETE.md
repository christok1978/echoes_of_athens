**3. Eleusis - Sanctuary of the Mysteries (ID: 262)**
- Location: 38.041100°N, 23.538000°E
- Panhellenic sanctuary of the Eleusinian Mysteries
- Most famous secret rites in ancient Greece for over 1,000 years

**4. Amphiareion of Oropos (ID: 263)**
- Location: 38.29139°N, 23.84556°E
- Sacred healing sanctuary with incubation stoa
- Major oracle and medical center of antiquity

**5. Rhamnous Fortress and Temple (ID: 261)**
- Location: 38.22333°N, 24.02722°E

- Remote coastal fortress guarding northeastern Attica

**6. Temple of Poseidon at Sounion (ID: 264)**
- Location: 37.6502°N, 24.02455°E
- Magnificent clifftop temple from Athens' golden age
- Beacon to sailors for 2,500 years

### Military Fortresses

**7. Phyle Fortress (ID: 266)**
- Location: 38.14°N, 23.637°E
- Birthplace of democracy's restoration (404-403 BC)
- Where Thrasyboulus began campaign against Thirty Tyrants

**8. Dekeleia - Spartan Stronghold (ID: 269)**
- Location: 38.1100°N, 23.7786°E
- Strategic fortress captured by Sparta in 413 BC
- Crippled Athens throughout final decade of Peloponnesian War

**9. Aphidna Fortress (ID: 271)**
- Location: 38.187100°N, 23.879200°E
- One of twelve original townships unified by Theseus
- Famous in myth as where Theseus hid Helen of Troy

**10. Oinoe Fortress (ID: 276)**
- Location: 38.168°N, 23.449°E
- Border stronghold guarding pass through Mount Cithaeron
- Besieged by Spartans at start of Peloponnesian War (431 BC)

**11. Aegosthena Fortress (ID: 279)**
- Location: 38.14750°N, 23.22861°E
- Tallest surviving defensive towers in ancient Greece
- Fortified port overlooking Corinthian Gulf

**12. Ancient Walls of Piraeus (ID: 270)**
- Location: 37.947500°N, 23.651200°E
- Mighty fortifications protecting Athens' port
- Famous Long Walls connecting Piraeus to Athens

### Ancient Demes & Townships

**13. Acharnae (ID: 265)**
- Location: 38.08194°N, 23.73147°E
- Largest deme of ancient Attica (3,000 hoplites)
- Famous charcoal burners and fierce warriors

**14. Paeania (ID: 267)**
- Location: 37.958506°N, 23.8552995°E
- Birthplace of Demosthenes, greatest Greek orator
- Eastern Hymettus slopes settlement

**15. Anagyrous (ID: 273)**
- Location: 37.83°N, 23.7976°E
- Southern coastal fishing deme
- Sanctuary to Mother of the Gods

**16. Aixone (ID: 277)**
- Location: 37.866334°N, 23.7548945°E
- Coastal deme with sanctuary to Hebe (modern Glyfada)
- Famous for fisheries in antiquity

**17. Prasiae (ID: 278)**
- Location: 37.8652°N, 24.0376°E
- Ancient port with excellent natural harbor (Porto Rafti)
- Departure point for sacred processions to Delos

### Natural & Industrial Sites

**18. Mount Lycabettus (ID: 274)**
- Location: 37.97778°N, 23.74167°E
- Highest point in Athens at 277 meters
- Chapel of St. George on site of ancient Zeus altar

**19. Mount Pentelikon Marble Quarries (ID: 275)**
- Location: 38.08083°N, 23.89972°E
- Source of marble for Parthenon and most Acropolis monuments
- Finest building stone in classical Greece

**20. Naval Base of Sounion (ID: 272)**
- Location: 37.6524°N, 24.0228°E
- Rock-cut ship sheds for warship maintenance
- Protected sea lanes to silver mines and Athens
