Athens in your pocket. History in your headphones.

Echoes of Athens is a free map-and-audio guide to 355 places in Attica — from the Parthenon to Parnitha, from Daphni Monastery to Lake Vouliagmeni. Open it in the browser. Walk. Listen.

How it works

Open the map. Allow GPS, or tap anywhere to simulate a walk. When you are near a pin, the story drawer opens with a photo, a spoken narration, a short quiz, and — at some sites — a local reward.

Install it as a home-screen app if you like. It is a Progressive Web App, so it works on iPhone, Android, and desktop without an app store.

Who it is for

  • First-time visitors who want more than a checklist of the Acropolis
  • Athenians exploring Attica on a weekend
  • Teachers and students building a self-paced field trip
  • Anyone who prefers walking with a story instead of a tour-bus loudspeaker

Share it in the street

Print this QR code on a hotel card, a cafe chalkboard, or a hiking kiosk. It opens the live guide at echoes-of-athens.vercel.app.
